# Bar model saved format

There is one bar-model format: schemaVersion **3**, rendererVersion **bar-model-3**,
styleProfile **lesson-v3**. Versions 1 and 2 were removed on 14 November 2026 (Tim's
decision: no backwards compatibility). Their files are no longer read: storage keeps the
original bytes of an unreadable draft and offers a download; the old `bar-model:v1` key is no longer looked at (Tim, 15 Nov). The past-paper examples (BM01–BM09, H07,
H18, source sharing) and the lesson examples R1–R5 were converted to this format and now use
its handwriting layout. Source capability does not establish production service or
intended-client adoption.

## Saved definition

Each bar stores `id`, `name:{text,state}`, exact nonnegative `offset`, and parts.
Each part stores `id`, positive exact `weight`, `labels:{inside,above?,below?}`
and one semantic `fill:none|neutral|blue|green|amber`. Every label is
`{text,state:value|blank|question|line|hidden}`. All five states retain entered
text; only value prints it. Question prints `?`; line prints an answer space.
Choosing a label position never moves or copies content. Bulk assignment is one
literal edit, with no continuing linkage or calculation.

An annotation has `id`, `scope`, `label`, `decoration:none|brace|dimension` and
`placement:above|below|inside|between`. Scope is either `{kind:"whole",barId}`
(follows the current whole bar) or `{kind:"range",from,to}` (fixed endpoint IDs).
Endpoints are `{barId,partId,edge:"start"|"end"}`. Between-bar annotations derive
their gap from adjacent endpoint bars. Reordering or removing bars never silently
selects another gap or boundary. Inside whole labels must fit a continuous interval
without meeting any divider; the editor keeps an invalid draft and offers outside
placement. Above/below annotations use deterministic first-fit lanes.

The optional title is a label. Up to three captions store stable `id`, `label`,
`alignment:left|centre|right` and `format:maths|plain` (default `maths`). Text is at
most 80 characters per label/line. Glyph advances and ink bounds come from the bundled
font files (`font-metrics.json`, regenerated by `scripts/bar-model-font-metrics.mjs`).
Unsupported visible characters fail with their code points; no replacement glyph is printed.

Presentation stores `unitMm:4..16`, `barHeightMm:8..24` (a requested minimum),
`fontPt:10..14`, `titlePt:12..18`, optional `nameWidthMm:8..60`, `output:grey|colour`
and optional `lines:"bold"`. Defaults are 10 mm/unit, 12 mm bars, 12 pt labels, 14 pt
title and greyscale. Bold prints every stroke 1.5 times as thick; `"normal"` is accepted on
input and never kept (U111). New models in the editor start from the house style chosen on
the booklet tools page.

## Working lines

Type `4/5 × 45 = 36`, `n/5 of 45 = 36`, `-3/8 × 48 = -18` or `1/2 + 3/4` in a Maths
caption: the whole statement uses horizontal fraction rules and one mathematical axis.
Fraction glyphs use the requested body size, without fit-dependent shrinking.

Fraction atoms are signed/unsigned numbers, single variables, coefficient terms,
or one parenthesised group, e.g. `(a+b)/c`. Nested fractions and ambiguous chains
such as `9/10/2026` fail with an explanation. Invalid drafts remain available and
exports stay blocked. Plain text is explicit for prose/dates/literal slashes;
failed Maths never selects it automatically. Titles and quantity names remain
plain text. Display layout never evaluates, solves, simplifies or fills answers.

## Writing geometry

Each explicit `state:line` reserves **12 mm above the upper stroke edge**, the
**0.25 mm** underline, and **3 mm below the lower stroke edge**. The underline is
**8 mm** long; the complete vertical footprint is **15.25 mm**. An invisible rectangle
controls layout; no writing box or shading is printed.

This covers inside/above/below section slots, whole/range/inside/between
annotations, names, titles and captions. Clear neighbours share a lane; overlapping
handwriting rectangles move lanes. A crossing guide fails explicitly.

Inside answer lines require uniform actual bars of at least **20 mm**. Outside lines
enlarge their own lanes. The editor shows minimum and actual height;
`printGuidance.referenceFeatures.tableRowHeightsMm` reports actual height.
Weights, offsets, boundaries and shared unit width never change to fit text.
Blank, question, hidden and zero remain distinct; hidden text never determines
pupil dimensions. Editable definitions/links still retain saved hidden answers.

## Saving

The browser key `maths-booklet-tools:bar-model:v2` holds `{storageVersion:1,envelope}`.
Reading is non-mutating. A corrupt record, or one of another version, enters recovery with
the original bytes protected; compare-before-write protects a newer tab.

## Output and limits

Browser and MCP share geometry and SVG. Check actual SVG, 600 ppi PNG, both clipboard
routes, JSON, original-SVG chunks, bundle and native vector consumption. Print at
Actual size / 100%; document resizing changes the physical handwriting space.

Limits: 6 bars, 24 parts/bar, 96 parts, 18 annotations, three captions, 30 operations,
32 KiB requests/definitions, 16,000-character browser editor links (MCP editor links
stop at 7,500 characters and fall back to the editable file), 280 × 260 mm, minimum 4 mm parts and
32 million 600 ppi pixels. Not every maximum can coexist. The dense fixture
(`stress-fixture.json`) has 96 inside answer lines at 20 mm actual bar height. Excess fails
explicitly without compressed handwriting, font shrinking or hidden scaling.

Examples live in `packages/bar-model-core/src/examples.json`; `docs/bar-model-examples/lesson-v3/`
holds the lesson sources. Reproduce native output with
`node scripts/bar-model-v3-review.mjs NEW_OUTPUT_DIRECTORY`; use `--write-examples` only for
deliberate website asset refreshes.
