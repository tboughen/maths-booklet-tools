# Frequency trees

`/tools/frequency-tree` draws exam-style frequency trees left to right: the total in the first oval, an oval for
each group with its count, and the group names on the branches or beside the ovals. It is wave 4 lane B (improvement
list N:P-2), approved by Tim on 10 November 2026 with this scope: a total, two or three branches, up to two levels,
any count left blank for pupils, hidden answers and an optional answer line for a probability question.

Code: `packages/frequency-tree-core` (schema, counts, layout, SVG, examples), `src/frequency-tree` (editor). The
layout and editor follow the [probability tree tool](PROBABILITY-TREE-TOOL.md). MCP access is the `frequency-tree` kind of the generic connector tools (`diagram_capabilities`,
`create_diagram`, `revise_diagram`; see [the diagram registry](DIAGRAM-REGISTRY.md)), adapted by
`packages/frequency-tree-core/src/interchange.ts`. Frequency trees can also be placed in worksheets.

## Document

- `total` is the count in the first oval. `nodes` is a flat list of groups; `parent: null` joins the total, sibling
  order is list order and every parent is listed before its children. A group with no children ends its path.
- 2 or 3 branches at each split, up to 2 levels (12 groups).
- Every printed value is a label `{ text, show }`. A hidden count is a saved answer: pupils see an empty oval and the
  teacher copy prints it. An empty shown count is a blank oval with no answer. Blank, zero and hidden stay distinct.
  A hidden group name leaves a dotted line.
- Counts are text: whole numbers are used by the explicit actions; anything else (such as `x` or `2x`) prints as typed.
- `labelPlace`: `branch` (names on the branches, as in November 2018 1F Q17) or `node` (beside the ovals, as in
  June 2018 2F Q14). `nodeShape`: `oval` or `box`. `linkLabels` (default on) makes a name edit apply to the same
  position across its level.
- Up to 4 questions print below the tree with an exam-style answer line. Each records the groups added for the top
  of the fraction and, optionally, the bottom (empty means the total), for the Calculate action only.

## Explicit actions only

Nothing is filled or calculated implicitly. Each is a button and is undoable:

- **Work out missing counts**: fills empty counts that follow from the others (a group is the sum of its branches;
  one empty branch is its group minus the rest), repeating until nothing more follows. Filled counts keep their
  Hide setting, so hidden answers stay hidden.
- **Calculate answer** for a question: writes the unsimplified fraction, such as 37/61.
- **Hide all counts except the total**, **Hide every count** and **Show all**.

Counts that are numbers but do not add up to their group give a teacher note only (deliberate error-spotting trees
are allowed). A negative count, overlapping names, or a tree wider than 18 cm or taller than 25 cm block export.

## Examples

Source-linked: November 2018 1F Q17 (coffee, with a given-that question), Mock Set 4 3F Q16 (lunches, every oval
including the total left for pupils), June 2018 2F Q14 (handedness, names beside the ovals) and November 2021 2F Q17
(text or email). Teaching example: three year groups by language, with a given-that question.
